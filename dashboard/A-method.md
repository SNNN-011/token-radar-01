# Method and evidence

## The universe

Read only the sealed DefiLlama snapshot. Keep the official watchlist as the authoritative coverage set, separate identified tokenized comparables, screen visible wrapped or bridged products and version duplicates, and retain a reason for every inclusion and exclusion. A missing token record is not proof of token absence. Parent-product relationships not supplied by the snapshot are not inferred.

## The estimate

Fit an implied circulating market cap from log TVL and category residuals of tokenized peers. Shrink offsets for supported categories; suppress the offset below the minimum peer count. Compare with flat and category-only market-cap/TVL baselines. Growth over seven days and listing age are displayed as context; their tested variants did not materially improve training folds under the frozen model-selection rule. TVL is not revenue, listing age is not protocol age, and this is not FDV.

## The benchmark

A deterministic slug-hash holdout is scored after training-only model selection and calibration. The same split is used for all displayed baselines. Final deployment coefficients use all eligible peers and are not the same coefficients as the scored holdout fit. Some tokenized peer categories cannot appear as candidates. The benchmark is cross-sectional, not a launch backtest; terms are not launch probabilities and the comparison is descriptive, not a significance claim. A wide, global empirical peer-residual band shows uncertainty, not precision. Positive signed log error means the actual market cap was above the estimate.

## The participation checks

Review the exact market, asset and chain, quote round-trip gas and fees, read the withdrawal path and delay, and stop when access, custody, price, or exit risk is unresolved. Source-backed routes cite publicly documented products; other candidates are marked verify terms or research only. Fees and eligibility can change after the snapshot. No rewards or investments are implied.
